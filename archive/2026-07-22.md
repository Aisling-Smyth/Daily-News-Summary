## 🇮🇪 Ireland

### Boyd found guilty of murdering Mark Carroll in Finglas

Derek Boyd was found guilty today of the murder of Mark Carroll in Finglas, Dublin. The jury determined that Boyd intentionally killed Carroll during an altercation on the night of June 26th, 2023. The prosecution presented evidence indicating a sustained assault occurred following a confrontation between the two men.

This verdict marks a significant moment in investigations into organized crime within Ireland and highlights the challenges of prosecuting individuals with prior convictions for violent offenses. Boyd's previous suspended manslaughter sentence raises concerns about accountability and the potential impact of such lenient sentences on public safety, prompting renewed scrutiny of sentencing guidelines related to serious assault cases.

### Two gardaí hospitalised after processing vape products

Two Garda officers were hospitalized following an incident while processing seized vape products at Edenderry Garda Station in County Offaly. The officers became unwell after handling the products, prompting immediate medical attention and the station’s temporary closure for decontamination procedures.

The closure of Edenderry Garda Station highlights concerns regarding the potential health risks associated with unregulated vaping products currently on the market. It also underscores the challenges faced by law enforcement in dealing with seized contraband and necessitates further investigation into the materials used in these products to determine the exact cause of illness and inform future preventative measures.

A man in his early thirties is due to appear in court following an incident at a church in Waterford earlier this week.
